Independent photo app for Proton Drive

A native photo library for Proton Drive.

Browse, back up, and search the photos in your Proton Drive on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Your photos stay end-to-end encrypted.

Requires an existing Proton account. Open source.

The same library on Mac, iPad, and iPhone.

Browse, view, and search your library.

Metal Grid

Years of photos in one grid.

The app draws the timeline with Metal, so scrolling stays smooth in a large library. Zoom the grid in or out to choose how many photos you see at once.

Viewer

Photos, videos, and Live Photos in full resolution.

Open a photo in full screen, move to the next one, and mark your favorites. The viewer also plays videos, Live Photos, and bursts.

Smart Search

Search that runs on your device.

Smart Search is optional. It indexes your photos on your device and does not upload photos for analysis. Your search queries stay on your device too. When you open Search, it suggests places, trips, seasons, favorites, and photo content from your own library.

An independent app, built on the Proton Drive SDK.

Encrypted Memories is an independent client for Proton Drive. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of its own.

Read the privacy policy
Sign-in
You sign in on Proton's page. The app never receives your Proton password.
Encryption
Your device encrypts and decrypts photos through the official Proton Drive SDK.
Data on your device
Thumbnails, offline photos, and the search index are encrypted at rest.
