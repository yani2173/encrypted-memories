# Recently Deleted

Recently Deleted contains media moved to Proton Drive trash.

You can:

- Review trashed photos and videos.
- Restore selected items to the library.
- Permanently delete items after confirmation when the backend permits it.

Photos you deleted before they were backed up appear here too, marked with a crossed-out cloud: they exist
only on this device. **Restore** puts them back into the backup. **Empty Recently Deleted** only removes them from the
list; they stay excluded from the backup and stay in Apple Photos or their folder. You can still bring them
back later under **Excluded from Backup** in the Backup settings.

Recently Deleted also opens offline and shows the last list the app received. Thumbnails of trashed photos
stay on the device while the photos are in the trash; the app loads them after every library thumbnail.

Copies that you merge in [[Duplicates|Duplicates]] also move here. Merging frees storage in Proton Drive only when Recently Deleted is emptied.

Removing an item from an album is not the same as moving it to Recently Deleted. Album removal keeps the item in the main library.

- **iPhone and iPad:** Open **Collections → Recently Deleted**.
- **Mac:** Select **Recently Deleted** in the sidebar.

Permanent deletion cannot be undone. Verify the selected items before confirming it.
