# Album Sync

Album Sync mirrors selected local Apple Photos albums into Proton albums. It uses the same shared synchronization controller on Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

## iPhone and iPad

Open Settings, choose **Album Sync**, and select local albums in the searchable picker.

## Mac

Open the Backup Settings tab and choose the local albums to synchronize.

When Album Sync backs up photos, **Use Cellular Data** is off, and the device uses cellular data, Album Sync shows **Waiting for Wi-Fi**.

Removing an album from the sync selection stops future synchronization. It does not delete the Proton album or its media. Photos permission is required, and limited Photos access can restrict the available local items.

Album Sync is separate from organizing existing Proton photos in [[Albums|Albums]].
